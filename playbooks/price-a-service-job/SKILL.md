# Price a service job without losing money

## When to use this

- The user is about to quote a job for a client
- The user's jobs keep ending with less money than expected
- The user asks how much to charge for a service visit

## The method, step by step

1. **See the job before quoting.** Never give a number from a phone call or one photo. Visit, or ask for a short video walk-through, and note anything that changes the work.
2. **List every cost.** Materials, parts, disposal, permits, tool wear and fuel, at what you will really pay this week.
3. **Count every hour.** Travel both ways, buying materials, setup, the work, cleanup and the follow-up call. Multiply by your hourly rate.
4. **Add 30% on top.** Add 30% to costs plus hours. It pays for the surprises, the quiet weeks and your profit.
5. **Send one clear price.** Say what is included and what is not, how long the price holds, and when payment is due.

## Rules and numbers

- Add 30% on top of costs and hours
- Every hour counts, travel included
- No price before seeing the job
- A quote holds for a fixed time, such as 14 days

## Mistakes to avoid

- Quoting on the phone, then paying the difference yourself
- Leaving out travel and shopping time
- Lowering the price instead of removing work
- Forgetting disposal or permit fees
